About Cyberl@ne26 2.0

Cyberl@ne26 2.0 is the annual national level symposium of the Department of Cybersecurity. Students from colleges across the region come together to compete, learn and build.

The day has four events: CTF, a hands-on workshop, Paper Presentation and Funmania. Every participant gets a certificate.
